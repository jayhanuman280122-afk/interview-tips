# Job Search Checklist

A simple, free checklist for anyone looking for work. Copy it, tick it off, share it.

## 1. Before you apply
- [ ] Reread the job description. Does the work match what you do?
- [ ] Find the company's official website yourself (don't use links in messages)
- [ ] Check the pay against similar roles
- [ ] Never pay for interviews, training or equipment

Not sure about a posting? Kaam Ki Khoj has a free [job check tool](https://kaamkikhoj.com/is-this-job-real).

## 2. Your CV
- [ ] Clear headline under your name (role + strongest skill)
- [ ] Bullets start with results, not duties
- [ ] Plain layout, one font, consistent dates
- [ ] Reordered for this specific job

No CV yet? You can [build a CV for free](https://kaamkikhoj.com/build-my-cv).

## 3. Interview prep
- [ ] Practise "tell me about yourself"
- [ ] Prepare two questions to ask at the end
- [ ] Test your video/audio setup for remote interviews

## 4. After you apply
- [ ] Keep a simple log of where and when you applied
- [ ] Follow up once, politely, after a week or so
